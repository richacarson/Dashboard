A YouTube video by Felix (mid-June 2026) presents a binary framework he calls the "Peace to Prosperity Pipeline" — arguing that the collision between a potential Iran War resolution and $1.8T in hidden AI debt creates either the greatest buying opportunity since 2020 or a portfolio-destroying trap, with virtually no middle ground. His framework: three forces (oil shock resolution, US/Europe rate divergence, institutional repositioning), five waves of capital flow (energy → transport → consumer → manufacturing → housing), and six sectors to position for.

Formal signing scheduled **June 19 in Switzerland.** Oil has crashed from $101.36 (June 3) to **$83.82 Brent** (June 15) — down 17% in 12 days. WTI near $80. **Felix's peace scenario is no longer hypothetical. Wave 1 (energy repricing) is happening right now.**

**Our assessment: Felix's framework went from "useful thought experiment" to "real-time playbook" in the 48 hours since the MOU was announced.** His five-wave capital flow sequencing is now the most relevant timing framework we have. The binary question has been answered — at least provisionally. But critical caveats remain: the MOU is unsigned, Iran says it's "still under negotiation," Israel refuses to withdraw from Lebanon, and mine clearance will take 40-50 days minimum. This could still collapse.

**What we should take from this:** The wave sequencing model is NOW ACTIVE for portfolio positioning. Our energy overweight faces immediate downside risk. The three portfolio gaps Felix identified (transport, REITs, European equities) are no longer theoretical — they're missed-opportunity risk if we don't have a plan.

## The Framework — What Felix Actually Says

### Three Forces

1. **Oil price resolution.** If the Iran War ends, oil drops from $108-112 to $60-70/bbl. Everything in the economy gets cheaper. Historical pattern: every major Middle East peace deal has triggered an oil drop within 90 days.

2. **US/Europe rate divergence.** The Fed is holding at 3.50-3.75% while the ECB just hiked. Most divided Fed vote in 30 years (8-3-1 split). Capital flows toward the US and away from Europe. Last time this happened (2014), US stocks outperformed European stocks for years.

3. **Confidence collision.** Every institutional model assumes war continues + oil stays high + Fed might hike. If the opposite happens, forced rebalancing creates violent price moves — and retail investors can move faster than institutions locked into quarterly rebalancing cycles.

### Five Waves of Capital Flow (Sequenced)

| Wave | Timing | What Happens |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Energy** | Immediate | Oil/gas producers reprice down; energy infrastructure benefits from restart |
| **2. Transport** | Weeks | Airlines, shipping, trucking see cost drops but maintain current pricing — margin explosion |
| **3. Consumer** | ~3 months | Gas savings flow to discretionary spending — restaurants, travel, retail surge |
| **4. Manufacturing** | 3-6 months | Lower input costs → US manufacturing competitive again (CAT, DE) |
| **5. Housing/Autos** | 6+ months | Fed can finally cut → mortgage rates drop → housing/auto boom |

### Six Sectors

Energy (shift producers → infrastructure), Transport/Airlines, Tech/AI, Consumer Discretionary, Real Estate/REITs, International/European equities.

## Fact Check — Where Felix Is Right and Wrong

| Claim | Verdict | Reality |
|---|---|---|
| **Fed vote 8-3-1, most divided in 30 years** | **Plausible** | June 2026 FOMC held at 3.50-3.75% with significant dissent. Our own research confirms the Fed is "boxed in" — can't cut (inflation from oil) and shouldn't hike (slowing economy) |
| **Fed balance sheet back above $6.7T** | **Needs context** | Balance sheet peaked at $8.9T in 2022 and has been declining via QT. If stabilized near $6.7T, the "stealth QE" framing is misleading — a pause in shrinking is not the same as printing money |
| **$1.8T in hidden AI debt** | **Inflated** | Moody's identifies ~$982B in off-balance-sheet commitments. Hyperscaler 2026 capex is $725B. Morgan Stanley does use the "AI subprime" comparison and flags hyperscaler gross leverage rising from 0.9x to 1.8x. But $1.8T conflates different metrics and time horizons |
| **Goldman Sachs forecasts $150 oil** | **False** | Goldman's base case is Brent $85-90/bbl for 2026, with a peak disruption scenario of $135. It was **Citi** that projected $150 in a bull-case Hormuz scenario. Felix attributed the wrong bank |
| **ECB hiked into a shrinking economy** | **Mostly true** | ECB raised 25bps on June 11 to 2.25%, while simultaneously lowering growth forecasts to 0.8%. Germany manufacturing PMI below 50 (contraction). Not a full eurozone recession yet, but hiking into clear weakness |
| **2014 divergence: US outperformed Europe by 30% over 2 years** | **Exaggerated** | The policy divergence is confirmed — Fed tapering while ECB easing. USD surged ~18% vs. euro. But the "30% outperformance" conflates nominal returns with currency effects. Early in the period, European stocks actually outperformed nominally |
| **Every Middle East peace deal triggers oil drop within 90 days** | **Directionally true, oversimplified** | 1991 Gulf War ceasefire and 2015 JCPOA both preceded oil declines, but attribution is complex. The 2014-2015 oil crash was primarily driven by the US shale boom and OPEC's November 2014 decision to maintain production — the JCPOA was one factor among many |
| **2020 recovery minted more millionaires than any 12-month period** | **Plausible** | S&P 500 gained ~75% from March 2020 bottom to March 2021. NASDAQ gained ~100%. Wealth creation was extraordinary |

**Summary: Felix gets the direction right on most macro trends but inflates the numbers and oversimplifies the causation.** For a YouTube audience this is fine. For a $516M portfolio, we need the real numbers.

## The Five-Wave Contingency Plan

If peace materializes (diplomacy restarts, ceasefire holds, Hormuz reopens), here's how Felix's wave model maps to portfolio actions:

### Wave 1: Energy Reprices — HAPPENING NOW

**Status:** Brent has dropped from $101.36 (June 3) to $83.82 (June 15) — down 17% in 12 days on the MOU announcement. WTI near $80. This wave is ACTIVE. CVX and VLO are repricing in real time.

**Action:** Take profits on CVX, VLO if the June 19 signing proceeds. Hold OKE, CNX, CTRA (midstream and gas have structural demand from data center buildout regardless of oil price). Hold VST (nuclear, not oil-sensitive).

**Why hold midstream:** KMI (on our buy list) and OKE benefit from volume regardless of price. Our Follow-the-Money research shows 70% of future data center power demand sits on KMI's pipeline footprint. Peace doesn't change that. In fact, Hormuz reopening = more gas flowing = more volume through pipelines.

**Critical timing:** Hormuz reopening is NOT instant. Only 2 ships/day currently transiting vs. 94 pre-war. Mine clearance estimates: 40-50 days per five Western maritime security sources (Reuters/Globe and Mail), up to 6 months per Pentagon briefing to Congress. Iran deployed at least 12-20 mines; it holds 2,000-6,000 in stockpile. Oil may stabilize $75-85 rather than crashing to Felix's $60-70 target — the physical reopening lag creates a floor.

### Wave 2: Transport Margins Explode (Weeks)

**Action:** This is our biggest gap. We'd need to act quickly. Possible entries: airline ETF (JETS) or individual names with strongest balance sheets. Shipping companies with locked-in contract rates would see pure margin expansion.

**Sizing:** Small. We don't know this space well, and airlines are structurally terrible businesses. A 2-3% portfolio allocation as a tactical peace trade, not a long-term hold.

### Wave 3: Consumer Spending Surges (~3 Months)

**Action:** Hold and potentially add to consumer-linked positions. SYF (consumer finance) goes from "underweight" to "hold." TOL goes from "reduce" to "watch for entry." Consumer discretionary names we don't own could be considered.

**Note:** This wave aligns with Q3 2026 earnings season. Our Oil Shock research says Q3 is when the DAMAGE hits in the war scenario. In the peace scenario, Q3 is when consumer RELIEF shows up instead. Same quarter, opposite outcome.

### Wave 4: Manufacturing Rebounds (3-6 Months)

**Action:** We're already positioned. CAT, STLD, FAST, PCAR are all exposed. Our Follow-the-Money names (GNRC, EME, HUBB) also benefit as manufacturing supply chains loosen.

### Wave 5: Fed Cuts → Housing/Autos Boom (6+ Months)

**Action:** This is the second gap. TOL becomes a buy instead of a sell. REIT exposure should be considered — commercial REITs that Felix calls "complete toxic garbage" would be the most asymmetric play if rates fall. Housing-adjacent names (home improvement, building materials) could work.

**Timeline reality check:** The Fed won't cut immediately on peace. Even in the best case, it takes 2-3 quarters for inflation to moderate enough to justify cuts. Wave 5 is a 2027 trade, not a July 2026 trade.

## Risks to Felix's Framework

1. **Binary framing ignores the middle.** The most likely outcome may be neither peace nor escalation — just a grinding status quo where oil stays $85-110 and the economy slowly absorbs the shock. Felix's framework has nothing to say about this scenario because it doesn't create dramatic capital flows.

2. **Peace ≠ instant oil drop.** Even if a ceasefire holds, Hormuz infrastructure needs months of maintenance and repair. Iran's production capacity has atrophied during sanctions. A 90-day oil drop from $110 to $60 is the optimistic end of the distribution, not the base case.

3. **The five-wave sequence isn't deterministic.** In 2015, oil crashed but consumer spending didn't boom because wage growth was flat. In 2020, the recovery was turbocharged by unprecedented fiscal stimulus ($5T+), not just cheap oil. Felix presents the waves as inevitable sequencing when they're actually conditional on multiple factors aligning.

4. **Felix is selling a workshop.** The binary framing, urgency language ("30 days to position"), and "watchers vs. builders" distinction are designed to drive signups for his Wealth Machine event, not to maximize analytical precision. Useful framework, but strip out the sales pitch.

5. **AI buildout changes the cycle.** Felix's framework is classical macro (oil → rates → sectors rotate). But $725B in committed hyperscaler capex creates a parallel demand cycle that doesn't depend on oil or rates. Our Follow-the-Money research captures this; Felix ignores it entirely.
