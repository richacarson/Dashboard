
# The Global Race to Build Oil Reserves: Supply Shock, Restocking Demand, and Family Capital Positioning

## Executive Summary

The 2026 Iran war (Feb 28 – June 17 ceasefire) round-tripped Brent from ~$71 pre-war to a ~$126 peak in April and back to ~$80 today. But the durable story isn't the spike — it's what the spike exposed: nearly every major importer was caught short, and the world is now in a **structural race to rebuild strategic reserves**. Reuters estimates this could pull **~500 million barrels of incremental demand** into the market over coming years — restocking demand layered on top of consumption, arriving *after* the spike and prolonging elevated prices.

This is the key distinction for positioning: the acute supply-shock trade (long oil into the disruption) is largely over. The forward trade is the **restocking floor** — a durable bid under crude as the US (SPR at a 43-year low), China (accelerating its >1.4B barrel reserve), and India (building from 8 days of cover) all buy simultaneously into a depleted market.

**The macro read-through is the bigger deal for the whole book:** the oil shock has frozen the Fed's 2026 rate cuts (Goldman now sees the first cut in mid-2027). That higher-for-longer regime is a headwind for the portfolio's largest dollar weight — the semiconductor/growth complex.

**Net assessment:** Family Capital is *thematically* well-hedged for higher oil (CVX, VLO, OKE, CTRA + AEM, FCX, NTR + GD, LMT), but **dollar-weighted net short** an oil shock because the semi complex (NVDA, AMD, TSM, MRVL) dominates the weight and de-rates in a risk-off/higher-rate regime. The single best risk-adjusted move: add AEM, trim TOL/PCAR, and pare the richest semis.

## Part 1: The Supply Shock — What Happened

### Conflict Timeline

| Date | Event |
|------|-------|
| Feb 28, 2026 | US/Israel launch ~900 strikes in 12 hours; Supreme Leader Khamenei killed |
| Mar 4 | Iran (IRGC) begins blocking Strait of Hormuz transit — mines, tanker attacks |
| Apr 13 | US imposes naval blockade of Iranian ports |
| Apr (late) | Brent peaks ~$126; Iran strikes Saudi East-West pipeline (-700k b/d) and UAE Fujairah |
| Jun 17 | Trump-Pezeshkian MoU signed at Versailles — ceasefire, Hormuz to reopen |
| Jun 20-22 | Implementation contested but Hormuz open in practice (record 55 ships/17M bbl one day) |

The IEA called this the largest supply disruption in oil-market history.

### Supply at Risk

- **Iranian exports:** ~2.0-2.1 mb/d pre-war, mostly halted by the blockade (China deliveries fell 38% vs. March)
- **Strait of Hormuz:** ~20 mb/d normally transits — **~20% of global petroleum consumption**, ~25% of seaborne oil trade. Q1 2026 flows fell ~30%
- **The OPEC+ spare-capacity trap:** ~4-5 mb/d of spare capacity exists (Saudi/UAE) — but it sits *behind* Hormuz. Bypass pipelines (Saudi East-West + UAE Fujairah) offer ~9 mb/d nameplate, only ~3-5 mb/d truly spare, and *both were attacked during the war*. Spare capacity **cannot bridge a full Hormuz closure**

### Price Action

```
Pre-war (Jan-Feb):  Brent ~$66-71  (already +$10 risk premium by mid-Feb)
Q1 close:           Brent ~$118
April peak:         Brent ~$126
Current (Jun 22):   Brent ~$80     (residual ~$10-15 war premium remains)
```

## Part 2: The Forward Story — The Reserve-Building Race

This is what the Reuters headline is about, and it's the more durable investment theme.

### Who Is Building, and How Much

| Country | Situation | Incremental Demand |
|---------|-----------|-------------------|
| **United States** | SPR at 43-year low; refilling | ~200M barrels over the next year |
| **China** | Already world's largest SPR (~1.4B bbl); accelerating | +266M bbl projected 2026 (~730k b/d) |
| **India** | Only ~8 days of cover; building toward IEA 90-day standard | 400M+ barrels needed (~$28B) |
| **Australia** | Chronically below 90-day IEA req | $7B program for 50+ days |
| **IEA bloc (32 members)** | Executed record 400M bbl coordinated release; must refill | 400M barrels |

Reuters' estimate: **~500M barrels of incremental demand** — roughly 5 days of global consumption pulled forward.

### The US SPR Situation

- **Current level: ~340.3M barrels (June 15, 2026) — lowest since 1983**, down ~75M bbl (18%) since the war began
- Trump authorized 172M barrel release starting March over ~120 days
- **Refill plan:** In-kind exchange to replace ~200M barrels within a year, with counterparties repaying 2026-2029 at an 18-24% in-kind premium. Energy Sec. Wright also seeking ~$20B to refill
- Must stay ~20% full to remain operational — limited room for further releases

### China as Strategic Leverage

- ~1.4B barrel reserve (largest in the world)
- Build rate accelerating: ~1.24M b/d in early 2026 vs. ~1.1M b/d in 2025
- Early-2026 crude imports up ~16% y/y, much directed to storage (including discounted Russian/Iranian barrels)

### Why This Matters for Price

Reserve building is **incremental demand layered on top of consumption** — barrels bought to sit in tanks, not burned. The dynamic: buffers are drawn down *into* the spike (bearish at the peak), then rebuilt *into* the recovery (bullish afterward), **extending the cycle and slowing the descent**. The US must refill ~200M bbl into a market where China and India are simultaneously accumulating. This creates a durable price floor.

### Sell-Side Scenario Matrix

| **Base** (Hormuz normalizes) | $80-90 (MS); mean-reverting | ~Market center of gravity |

Polymarket prices **~90% odds of full traffic normalization by year-end 2026** — i.e., the market is in the "moderate, slow-recovery" zone, treating the $150-180 closure tail as low-probability.

### The Macro Read-Through (This Affects the Whole Book)

- **Inflation:** +$10/bbl sustained ≈ **+0.3-0.4 pp headline CPI** (Fed FEDS Notes / Scotiabank). A move to $120 implies ~+1.2-1.5 pp
- **Fed policy:** The spike has repriced the cut path hard. Futures now imply only **one 2026 cut** (vs. two pre-war); Goldman **pulled all 2026 cut calls**, pushing the first cut to **mid-2027**. PCE seen accelerating toward 3.5% y/y
- **The bind:** Classic supply-shock stagflation dynamic — lifts inflation while sapping growth, splitting the Fed's dual mandate. Rate-cut optionality is effectively on hold

### Historical Pattern

| Episode | Move | Duration | Lesson |
|---------|------|----------|--------|
| 1973 embargo | $3 → $12 (4x) | Years | Created the US SPR — reserve-building as response is the original template |
| 1990 Gulf War | $17 → $42 (~2.5x) | ~9 months, reversed fast | Pure fear spikes self-correct once supply threat removed |
| 2003 Iraq | Fell on invasion, then climbed to $147 by 2008 | Multi-year | Spikes atop *tight inventories + rising demand* persist |
| 2022 Russia/Ukraine | $93 → $139 peak | ~5 months sustained | Structural supply removal lasts quarters |

**2026 resembles 2003→2008:** a real supply cut *plus* simultaneous global restocking *plus* a depleted US SPR. That argues for a **more durable price floor and slower normalization** than a fear-only event.

Family Capital is well-hedged thematically but carries a hidden vulnerability: the semiconductor concentration means the book is dollar-weighted net short an oil shock through the rate channel.
