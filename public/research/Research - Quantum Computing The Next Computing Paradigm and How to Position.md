## Thesis

Quantum computing has crossed from physics lab to commercial reality. IonQ became the first quantum company to exceed $100M in annual revenue (FY2025: $130M, +202% YoY), the U.S. government committed **$2 billion via the CHIPS Act** to nine quantum companies on May 21, 2026, and Quantinuum IPO'd on June 4 at a **$14B valuation** — the largest quantum IPO in history. The parallels to our humanoid robotics research are striking: massive TAM, pre-revenue pure-plays trading at extreme multiples, a handful of big tech players building platforms, and a supply chain where the real picks-and-shovels opportunity lives. The critical difference: **seven of Family Capital's current holdings already have quantum exposure** — and the post-quantum cryptography mandate creates a second, more immediate investment thesis alongside the hardware buildout.

## The Technology Landscape — Five Approaches Competing

| Approach | Leaders | Qubits (Current) | Two-Qubit Fidelity | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Superconducting** | IBM, Google, Rigetti | ~156 qubits | 99.5-99.9% | Fastest gate speeds (~ns); most mature; leverages semiconductor manufacturing | Requires dilution refrigerators at ~15mK; short coherence times; expensive cooling |
| **Trapped Ion** | IonQ, Quantinuum | ~98 qubits | **>99.9% (best)** | Highest fidelity; longest coherence; all-to-all connectivity | Slower gate speeds (~ms); harder to scale qubit count; complex laser systems |
| **Photonic** | PsiQuantum, Xanadu | ~216 qubits | In development | Room-temperature; natural for networking; potential massive scale | Probabilistic gates; photon loss; fidelity not yet competitive |
| **Neutral Atom** | QuEra, Pasqal | ~6,100 atoms | ~99.5% | Highest qubit counts; rapid scaling; reconfigurable | Mid-pack fidelity; fewer commercial deployments |
| **Topological** | Microsoft | ~8 qubits | Not benchmarked | If it works, inherent error protection — could leapfrog everyone | Least mature; existence of Majorana fermions still debated |

**Key takeaway:** Trapped ion (IonQ, Quantinuum) currently offers the best balance of fidelity and usability. Superconducting (IBM, Google, Rigetti) has the broadest ecosystem. Neutral atom scales fastest. This is analogous to the humanoid robotics modality question — no winner yet, and the "picks and shovels" play avoids the modality bet entirely.

## Recent Breakthroughs — Why 2026 Is the Inflection

| Breakthrough | Company | Significance |
|---|---|---|
| **Exponential error reduction** | Google (Willow, Oct 2025) | First proof that adding qubits reduces error rates exponentially. Ran 13,000x faster than world's best supercomputer |
| **Logical qubits outperform physical** | Quantinuum (2026) | 94 error-protected logical qubits; error rates 10-100x lower than physical qubits. Industry first — "beyond break-even" |
| **99.99% two-qubit gate fidelity** | IonQ (2025) | World record fidelity. #AQ 64 on Tempo system, three months ahead of schedule |
| **Topological qubit chip** | Microsoft (Majorana 1, Feb 2025) | First QPU built on topological qubits. If validated, eliminates heavy error correction overhead |
| **Cat qubit error suppression** | Amazon (Ocelot, 2025) | Reduces error correction costs by up to 90%. Cuts quantum computing timeline by ~5 years |
| **$2B CHIPS Act commitment** | U.S. Government (May 2026) | IBM: $1B, GlobalFoundries: $375M, D-Wave: $100M, Rigetti: $100M, plus 5 others |

The error correction breakthroughs are the key unlock. Quantum computers have been theoretically powerful for decades — the barrier was that errors compounded faster than computation could proceed. Google Willow and Quantinuum's logical qubit demonstrations prove that scalable error correction is feasible. This is the equivalent of the transformer architecture breakthrough for AI — the moment the technology became practically achievable.

## The Post-Quantum Cryptography Mandate — The More Immediate Thesis

### Why This Matters Now

While fault-tolerant quantum computing is 5-10 years away, the **cryptography threat is functionally immediate**:

- **"Harvest Now, Decrypt Later":** Adversaries are stealing encrypted data TODAY to decrypt when quantum capability matures. This makes the threat timeline immediate for long-shelf-life data (government secrets, medical records, financial data)
- **NIST finalized post-quantum cryptography (PQC) standards** on August 13, 2024: ML-KEM (encryption), ML-DSA (signatures), SLH-DSA (hash-based signatures)
- **NIST IR 8547 deprecates RSA-2048 and ECC P-256 by 2030, removes them entirely by 2035**
- Every organization handling sensitive data must migrate to quantum-safe encryption within the next 4-9 years
- **Q-Day estimates:** Expert consensus puts a cryptographically relevant quantum computer at 10-15 years; aggressive estimates say 3-5 years. Qubit requirements dropping fast — from 20M physical qubits (2019 estimate) to potentially <100K (2026 Iceberg Quantum proposal)

## Risks to This Analysis

1. **Quantum winter.** If error correction breakthroughs stall, the sector could enter a multi-year winter similar to AI's 2017-2022 period. Pure-play stocks would collapse. Supply chain impact would be minimal
2. **Jensen was right.** NVIDIA's CEO said quantum is "15-30 years away" from practical use. If the timeline is that long, current valuations are unjustifiable and even picks-and-shovels benefit is negligible
3. **China leapfrogs.** China's 15th Five-Year Plan targets quantum leadership with massive state funding. If Chinese quantum companies win, Western pure-plays lose — but the supply chain (KEYS, AMD, NVDA) still benefits
4. **PQC migration delays.** If the 2030 RSA deprecation deadline gets pushed back, the FTNT/NXPI tailwind weakens
5. **Modality risk.** We don't know which approach wins. Trapped ion, superconducting, photonic, neutral atom, and topological are all in play. Picks-and-shovels avoids this bet; pure-play investors face existential modality risk
6. **The $931M insider selling signal.** When the people building these companies are net sellers at these prices, that's a data point worth respecting
